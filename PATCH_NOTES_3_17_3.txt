Space Survivors — Patch 3.17.3 combat curve
===========================================
1. PRIMARY CANNONS — intentional (every hull has a base gun; ship identity is the
   projectile count 1-3 plus the signature weapon) but they were an unintended build
   core: full `damage` per shot at a 0.32 s cycle (~39 DPS per barrel from second one,
   more than any L1 weapon) and every system rode on them (explosive, burn, cryo, homing).
   Now 55% damage, smaller bolts, and no explosive/burn/cryo riders. Homing still works.
2. EARLY POWER SPIKE — the cannons above, plus the fixed enemy population cap of 24
   (spawn throttled from 18 alive). Once a build cleared 24 enemies faster than they
   spawned there was nothing left to threaten the ship, from about minute 3.
3. PRESSURE — population cap now grows 24 -> ~80 by 10:00 (+1 per 10.5 s), spawn interval
   floor 0.46 -> 0.30 s, 4 per spawn after 6:00, and a standard-mode elite every 75 s
   from 4:00. No enemy damage values were raised.
4. BOSS — flat 5200 HP, no burst limit, every weapon bonus applied. Now: HP x(1 + 0.10 per
   player level) (level 18 -> ~14.5k), no single hit removes more than 2.5% of hull, and
   each phase transition raises a 2.6 s shield with a 14-orb radial burst the player must
   weave through. Representative late build (4 L5 weapons incl. Dreadnought Salvo, 6
   damage, 5 fire rate): ~65-75 s to kill instead of 5-10 s.
5. OUTLIERS — controlled L5 tests from 3.17.2 stand (Railgun 1392, Scattergun 1357,
   Mines 1673, Missiles 1688, Torpedo 1384, Lance 1512, supers 1477-2469). The 48.5%
   cannon share is gone with (1).
6. HORN SOUNDS — three tonal low-frequency sources remained: big_boom (42 Hz sine tail,
   every torpedo / singularity detonation — the one you kept hearing with Torpedo Bay),
   phase (900 -> 0 Hz descending sweep, Phase Disruptor / elite magnet drop) and boss_roar
   (48+73 Hz sines). All rebuilt noise-based: impact crack + short sub punch, digital
   chirp, mechanical roar.
