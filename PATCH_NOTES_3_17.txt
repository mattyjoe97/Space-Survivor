Space Survivors — Patch 3.17 "Progression Overhaul"
===================================================
Weapon roster (14) and superweapons (7) unchanged. Ships, enemies, sectors, audio,
VFX, saves, pickups and aim modes untouched. Enemy HP/spawn scaling untouched.

AUDIT (what was wrong, measured with tests/ProgressionSim.tscn)
- XP curve 15+lvl^1.3*6.5 vs ~16x growth in XP income -> 10 level-ups in the last
  2 minutes (lvl 23 @7:00). Late choices were cheap.
- A new-weapon card was guaranteed on every level-up while a slot was open ->
  4 slots full by minute 3, no later weapon decisions.
- Core stats: 10 levels x up to 25%/pick multiplicative (Fire Rate cap = 18x).
  That was the minute 2-4 power spike.
- Legendary = 5x Common; Luck shifted up to 27 pts of rarity weight.
- A reroll was granted on EVERY level-up (cap 3) -> effectively unlimited.
- Merchant: one visit, static 8 items, flat prices, unlimited stat purchases.
- Scrap variance 5x (contracts 120-400, Salvage relic +50%).
- 3 of 7 systems were radius stats; explode_damage was dead without explode_on_hit.
- Relics were a forced single card. Superweapons never happened in 7-minute sims.

STRUCTURE
Weapons   4 slots (5 with meta Arsenal, was 6), cap 5. New-weapon cards are
          guaranteed until the 2nd weapon, then compete (50% / 35%) with upgrade
          cards. Owned weapons weigh 3 + 0.4/level; the synergy partner of an
          owned weapon gets +1.5 + 0.6/level, so a committed pair reaches a
          superweapon around level 12-14 (~5-6 min) without being forced.
Systems   9 systems, 3 tiers each, no rarity, max 4 distinct. Compatibility is
          re-checked on every draw (also for owned systems).
          extra_projectile / pierce / area / proj_size / explosive (merged
          explode_on_hit + explode_damage) / duration / homing / incendiary /
          cryo_rounds. The last four change behaviour through the weapon
          framework (duration_mult, homing_tier, burn_tier, cryo_tier).
Core      Per-stat step x per-stat cap, max 6 distinct:
          Damage +8% x6 | Fire Rate +6% x5 | Hull +12% x5 | Speed +6% x4
          Crit +5% x5 | Proj. Speed +12% x3 | Magnet +25% x3 | XP +10% x3
          Scrap +15% x3 | Luck +4 x4.  (Fire Rate ceiling 1.34x, was 18x.)
Rarity    Multiplies the step: 1.0 / 1.35 / 1.75 / 2.25 / 3.0. Caps count picks.
          Base odds 70/20/7/2.5/0.5. Luck (cap 40) moves 0.5 pt per Luck out of
          Common -> at max Luck 50/28/13/6/3. Rarity applies to core stats only.
Level-up  3 cards: weapon opportunity / build-relevant system / core stat.
          Relic offered as a CHOICE at 6/12/18/24. Rare Blueprint (all core
          cards >= Rare) at 9. Rerolls use the same builder.
Rerolls   2 per run, no refresh, +1 purchasable once at the Merchant. Cap 3.
XP        xp_to_next = 22 + lvl^1.42 * 6.2 (first level 26). Per-enemy XP grows
          3.5 + 0.018t (was 0.025t) so kill-bound income snowballs less.
Scrap     Drop chance 28% (was 20%), 3-6 per drop (was 2-5). Contract rewards
          halved. Expected ~250 by visit 1, ~600 by visit 2, ~1000 by visit 3.
Merchant  Three visits (2:30 / 5:30 / 8:30). Per visit: Weapon Crate (new
          weapon if a slot is open, else a level for an owned one), a System
          module, two core stat items rolled >= Uncommon (count against caps),
          one utility (repair 40% / barrier / 6s magnet / reroll token once per
          run), a second repair if hull < 60%, and from visit 2 a 55% chance of
          a Black Market relic. Base costs 220/180/170/110+ scrap, x(1+0.45 per
          visit), every purchase raises the remaining prices 20%. One buy per
          offer. "New Stock" refresh once per visit (60 scrap scaled).
Relics    One-time, non-rankable, offered as a choice at milestones and in the
          Black Market. Unchanged effects.

EXPECTED RUN (10 min, normal build): level ~20; 4 weapons with 2 maxed (1
superweapon if committed); 3-4 systems; 10-14 core picks over 4-6 stats;
1-2 relics; 6-9 merchant purchases; 2-3 rerolls.

  scrap earned / spent @ 7:00                      174-913/360  614/560

The 2-4 minute spike is gone for normal builds; strong AoE builds still pull
ahead (they should) but stay within ~2x instead of ~5x. The pilot AI dies
around 5:00 in most seeds regardless of system (it did in the baseline too), so
late-game numbers come from the seeds that survived.
