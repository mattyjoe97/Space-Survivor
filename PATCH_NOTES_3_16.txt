Space Survivors — Patch 3.16 "Fourteen Weapons, Seven Superweapons"
====================================================================

The weapon system has been rebuilt from scratch on top of 3.15. Ships, passives,
enemies, sectors, economy, saves, menus, aim modes and the audio framework are
untouched. Solar Flare and the eight old stat-bonus "synergies" are gone.

ARCHITECTURE
- scripts/weapons/WeaponBase.gd   shared helpers: aim (manual/auto), line & radius
                                   queries, crit + status-aware hit(), damage attribution
- scripts/weapons/WeaponManager.gd one module per weapon under Player/Weapons;
                                   activates superweapons and suppresses their parents
- scripts/weapons/StatusHost.gd    burn / irradiated / phased / chilled effects that
                                   attach to ANY enemy (elites and boss included)
                                   and draw their own overlays
- scripts/weapons/WeaponFX.gd      lightning bolts, slewing beams, pulse rings
- scripts/weapons/fx/*.gd          per-weapon world objects (mines, torpedoes, coils,
                                   flame cone, phase wave, rifts, black hole...)
- Projectile.gd gained split-on-hit, ricochet, knockback and on_hit callbacks.
- Level-up cards now show what the NEXT level actually changes for each weapon.

THE 14 WEAPONS (role · behaviour · L5)
 1 Hunter Missiles   ORDNANCE  volleys swing wide then curve in; L3 warheads;
                               L5 split into two micro-seekers on impact
 2 Plasma Lance      ENERGY    sustained beam that sweeps onto the target and burns;
                               L5 refracts off the first enemy into a second beam
 3 Arc Coil          ENERGY    jagged chain lightning, micro-stun per jump;
                               L5 leaves an overcharge node that re-arcs
 4 Void Blades       VOID      always-on orbiting crescent blades;
                               L5 periodic outward lash to double range
 5 Railgun           KINETIC   visible charge-up, then a screen-crossing rail with
                               knockback; L5 fires perpendicular cross-rails
 6 Graviton Mines    ORDNANCE  drift -> arm (blink) -> drag -> detonate;
                               L5 collapse into a singularity first
 7 Radiation Field   AURA      toxic field; irradiated enemies keep ticking after they
                               leave; L5 fallout cloud on death
 8 Cryo Field        CONTROL   frost pulses slow and build freeze; frozen enemies take
                               +25%; L5 shatter on death chills neighbours
 9 Side Batteries    KINETIC   visible flank turrets fire alternating broadside bursts
                               perpendicular to heading; L5 quad batteries (4 sides)
10 Tesla Coil        ENERGY    drops a stationary coil turret with uptime bar that zaps
                               everything in range; L4 two coils; L5 coils link a wall
11 Plasma Flamethrower ENERGY  continuous animated flame cone, stacking burn;
                               L5 leaves burning pools
12 Torpedo Bay       ORDNANCE  slow heavy torpedoes accelerate into a huge knockback
                               blast; L5 burst into 3 cluster bomblets
13 Photon Scattergun KINETIC   close-range pellet spray that ricochets into new targets;
                               L5 double ricochet + double-tap
14 Phase Disruptor   VOID      crescent wave passes through enemies and marks them to
                               take +15..30% damage from everything; L3 shove;
                               L5 marks collapse into pulsing rifts

THE 7 SUPERWEAPONS (both parents at level 5)
 Hunter Missiles + Torpedo Bay      DREADNOUGHT SALVO   rolling 8-torpedo homing salvo,
                                                        launcher racks open on the hull
 Plasma Lance + Flamethrower        HELLFIRE LANCE      wide flaming beam sweeping a full
                                                        circle, leaving fire pools
 Arc Coil + Tesla Coil              STORM GRID          four orbiting tesla nodes linked by
                                                        live lightning walls
 Void Blades + Phase Disruptor      DIMENSIONAL REAPER  two huge phase scythes; every cut
                                                        opens a rift that drags neighbours
 Railgun + Photon Scattergun        STARBREAKER         1s charge, colossal rail that sprays
                                                        photon shrapnel sideways
 Graviton Mines + Cryo Field        FROZEN SINGULARITY  persistent black hole ahead of the
                                                        ship; freezes, drags, collapses
 Radiation Field + Side Batteries   NUCLEAR BROADSIDE   quad fission shells leave fallout
                                                        zones; aura blooms to reactor size
On activation: full-screen ring/ray burst, "SUPERWEAPON ONLINE" banner with the
weapon's description, unique rising fanfare, and the two parent chips in the HUD
are replaced by a single gold-bordered superweapon chip with a fused icon.

ICONS / AUDIO
- New vector icons for Tesla Coil, Plasma Flamethrower, Torpedo Bay, Photon
  Scattergun, Phase Disruptor, and fused "super" icons for all seven.
- New SFX: torpedo, scatter, flame, phase, tesla, big_boom, superweapon.

FIXES
- VFX.shockwave scaled its Line2D, which multiplied the stroke width into a giant
  disc on every explosion. Rewritten to regenerate points; explosions are now rings.
- Enemy chill/slow now goes through a proper chill_timer on Enemy.
