PHASE 3.4.4 - AOE POWER PASS

- Increased sustained DPS for Ion Burst, Graviton Mines and Solar Flare.
- Void Blades now have a wider hit envelope, more blades, faster rotation and much stronger contact damage.
- AoE weapons receive a modest 12-18% damage bonus against elite enemies so close-range builds can stay relevant in elite encounters.
- Increased uptime on all major area weapons without increasing their base radius again.
- Graviton Mines retain their randomized scatter and control-zone behavior.
- Visual impact increased slightly for Void Blades, Ion Burst and Solar Flare.
- Ranged weapons were left unchanged; this pass is specifically intended to bring area/control archetypes up to a competitive damage floor.
