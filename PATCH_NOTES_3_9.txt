Phase 3.9 - Context-Aware Systems

- Systems are now offered only when at least one currently equipped weapon can use them.
- System compatibility is data-driven by weapon ID rather than loose display strings.
- Level-up system cards show the currently compatible equipped weapons; the full compatibility details are in the tooltip.
- Tooltips explain the system's weapon-specific behavior without forcing non-projectile weapons into projectile mechanics.
- Extra Projectile now applies only to compatible discrete-attack weapons: Hunter Missiles, Plasma Lance, Void Blades, Graviton Mines, and Side Batteries.
- Plasma Lance, Hunter Missiles, Void Blades, Graviton Mines, and Side Batteries now actually receive their extra attacks from Extra Projectile.
- Area, Aura Size, Projectile Size, Pierce, and Explosion systems use the tighter weapon compatibility model.
- Radiation Field and Micro Singularity remain area effects and are not treated as projectile weapons.
- Removed stale compatibility references to removed Drone Swarm/Ion Burst behavior.
