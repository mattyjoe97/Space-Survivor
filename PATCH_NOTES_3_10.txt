SPACE SURVIVORS — PHASE 3.10
CRYO FIELD WEAPON SWAP

- Replaced Micro Singularity as a run weapon with Cryo Field.
- Cryo Field pulses continuously even when no enemies are inside the field.
- Each pulse deals damage and builds freeze exposure on enemies.
- Enemies freeze after a cumulative exposure threshold.
- Base freeze threshold is 2.65 seconds of exposure, scaling down with weapon level to a minimum of 1.45 seconds.
- Normal enemies freeze for 1.10 seconds.
- Elite enemies freeze for 0.78 seconds so control remains meaningful without permanently locking elites.
- Cryo Field radius starts at 155 and scales by +14 per weapon level.
- Pulse cadence starts near 0.52 seconds and improves to 0.26 seconds at higher levels.
- Extra Projectile does not apply to Cryo Field.
- Area and Aura Size systems are compatible with Cryo Field.
- Added two new superweapon combinations:
  FROZEN VOID — Cryo Field + Void Blades.
  NUCLEAR WINTER — Radiation Field + Cryo Field.
- Frozen Void increases Cryo Field impact and improves elite control while Void Blades shred frozen targets.
- Nuclear Winter adds an additional radiation burn to Cryo Field pulses.
- Removed stale Micro Singularity upgrade/evolution references from the active weapon pool.
- Retained the Singularity ship frame as a separate ship identity; only the run weapon was replaced.
