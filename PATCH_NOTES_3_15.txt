Space Survivors — Patch 3.15 "Polish & Presentation"
=====================================================

ICONS (new)
- scripts/GameIcons.gd: procedural vector icon library. Distinct icons for
  every weapon (10), every ship (9), every enemy incl. elites / commander /
  boss (12), and ~20 misc glyphs (XP, scrap, heal, luck, reroll, merchant,
  relic, cursed, synergy, systems, stat upgrades...). Crisp at any size.
- scripts/IconView.gd: drop-in Control badge -> IconView.make(kind, id, color, px)
- Icons now appear on: HUD weapon chips (with level pips), level-up cards,
  hangar ship cards, the HUD ship badge, and the Codex (enemies + synergies).

SHIPS
- Every ship has its own in-game hull (previously all shared one hull with
  different colors). Hulls match the hangar preview silhouettes.
- Animated engine flames scale with throttle; ship banks/skews into turns;
  blinking running lights; canopy specular sweep.
- Damage state: sparks + smoke wisps below 45% hull.
- Shield shimmer ring while invulnerable (dash / post-hit).
- Engine particle trail (CPUParticles2D) and dash afterimage ghosts.

ENEMIES
- Per-variant redesigns: snapping mandibles, rotating armor plates (Armored),
  charge lights that fill as the shot readies (Sniper), glowing plasma maw
  during telegraph (Void Spitter), phasing core (Teleporter), split seam
  (Splitter), pulsing tendrils (Leecher).
- Eye/sensor tracks the player.
- Hit flash + squash-and-stretch on damage (the old flash targeted a hidden
  polygon and never showed). Elites and the Void Titan also flash on hit.
- Freeze build-up shows ice crystals; full freeze tints the body.

VFX / PARTICLES
- New helpers in VFX.gd: sparks(), smoke(), shockwave(), debris(),
  muzzle_flash(), enemy_death(), ship_ghost(), mega_explosion(), postfx_punch().
- Hits spawn spark streaks; crits add directional sparks.
- Explosions now layer shockwave + sparks + smoke.
- Enemy deaths: flash + shockwave + sparks + tumbling shards (+ smoke for heavies).
- Elite and boss deaths use a cinematic multi-stage mega explosion.
- Muzzle flashes on primary / side guns / missiles; missile exhaust particles.
- Projectiles restyled: energy bolts vs. finned missiles, curved history trails.
- Level-up, dash, Solar Flare, elite spawn and player death all got bigger.
- Screen shake fixed (camera lookup was failing silently) and now decays
  over several steps.

POST-PROCESSING & BACKGROUND
- shaders/postfx.gdshader + scripts/PostFX.gd: vignette, chromatic
  aberration punch on hits / railgun / boss events, low-hull red edge pulse,
  faint film grain. HUD stays crisp (UI layer moved above PostFX).
- shaders/nebula.gdshader + scripts/NebulaBackdrop.gd: procedural fbm nebula
  with twinkling stars, parallaxes with the camera, palette shifts per sector.
- New Settings toggle: "Screen Effects (vignette / distortion)" (saved).

AUDIO
- 15 new procedural SFX: arc zap, railgun, solar flare, void blades, mines,
  freeze, merchant, boss roar, low-hull warning, synergy, evolution,
  UI hover, UI confirm, shield/vacuum, elite spawn. All wired in.
- New MusicManager autoload: procedural looping music synthesized once in a
  background thread (~2s), three phase-locked stems (pad / drive / tension)
  crossfaded between moods: menu, calm (run start), combat (after 18s),
  boss. Music ducks under the level-up / merchant / pause panels.
  Uses the existing Music volume slider.

UI ANIMATION
- Level-up cards stagger-slide in, panel pops, cards scale on hover with
  glowing icon; HUD weapon chips pop in when acquired.
- All themed buttons (menu, hangar, merchant) get hover/press sounds and a
  small scale pop.
- HUD weapon panel only rebuilds when the loadout changes (was every frame).

FIXES
- Enemy death spawns (gems, scrap, splitter children, pickups) are now
  deferred, removing "Can't change this state while flushing queries" errors.

TESTING
- tests/SmokeTest.tscn: headless run that spawns every enemy, gives every
  weapon, kills elites and the boss, opens level-up + merchant, and kills
  the player. Run: godot --headless --path . res://tests/SmokeTest.tscn
