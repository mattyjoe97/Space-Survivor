PHASE 3.6 — WEAPON IDENTITY, BUILD RULES & GAME FEEDBACK

• Radiation Field, Micro Singularity, and Side Batteries are now full WEAPONS and consume weapon slots.
• Weapon choices do not use rarity.
• Systems are filtered to only appear when at least one currently equipped weapon supports them.
• Enemy body collisions no longer trap/circle the player; contact damage still uses the player hurtbox.
• Merchant direction arrow + distance added to HUD.
• Elite vacuum-magnet drops are hardened and their pickups remain collectible by the magnet area.
• Arc Coil + Graviton Mines now visibly and mechanically activate as GRAVITY STORM.
• End-of-run damage chart is now visual and ranked, with percentage bars.
• Upgrade values were raised and differentiated by stat, including stronger Magnet/Area/Damage/Fire Rate rolls.
• XP curve is slightly easier in mid/late levels.
