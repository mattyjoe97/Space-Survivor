Space Survivors — Patch 3.14
============================

Performance
- Cached enemy list in GameManager (refreshed ~12×/sec) and routed Player / Projectile / Spawner queries through it. Removes dozens of full-group scans per frame during combat.
- Cached EnemyArt node on enemies (no per-frame get_node_or_null).
- Damage numbers now hard-cap at 28 concurrent instances (crits/heals still always show). Reduces Label spam under heavy AoE.

Audio
- New AudioManager autoload with fully procedural SFX (no external assets required).
- Effects routed to the existing Effects bus so the in-game volume sliders work.
- Sounds added for:
  • primary / side gun shots, missiles, plasma lance
  • hits, crits, explosions
  • enemy / elite / boss deaths and boss hits
  • XP, scrap, and heal pickups
  • dash, player damage, level-up jingle
- Light rate-limiting so dense combat stays clear instead of white-noise.

Notes
- Procedural tones are intentionally short and punchy; they scale with the Effects volume slider.
- Enemy population still hard-capped near 24 for stable frame times.
